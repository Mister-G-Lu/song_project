## For the Prediction Model

### Feature Hierarchy (by predictive power)

1. **Genre** — Soundtrack, Classical, Eurovision = strong positive; Rap, R&B, Indie = strong negative
2. **Artist familiarity** — 1.86x enrichment for artists with 10+ songs
3. **Artist-relative rating** — is this song exceptional for THIS artist? (the "This Love" effect)
4. **Release year** — 2015-2020 peak, but confounded with listening period
5. **Exclude:** mumble rap, RYM default-90s, genre-mislabeled entries

### Recommended Next Steps

1. **Enrich with Spotify audio features** (energy, danceability, valence, acousticness, instrumentalness) — these are the "what does it sound like" features that genre labels miss
2. **Build the 90+ classifier** — binary prediction: "Will this become a favorite?"
3. **Cluster the 90+ songs** — discover your taste modes (cinematic, vocal showcase, theatrical, etc.)
4. **Find 90+ outliers** — songs that defy the model's prediction — these reveal your hidden preferences
