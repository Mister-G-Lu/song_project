## For the Prediction Model

### What Actually Predicts 90+

1. **Distinctive creative identity** (genre is a proxy, not the cause)
2. **Artist familiarity** (1.86x enrichment for 10+ song artists)
3. **Artist-relative rating** (is this song exceptional for THIS artist?)
4. **Specific sub-characteristics within genres** (orchestration + dynamic contrast for Soundtrack; vocal showcase for Pop; lyrical complexity for Rap)

### What Doesn't Predict 90+

1. Genre alone (Country avg 67.5 but 8.1% 90+ rate)
2. Artist reputation (Maroon 5 avg 66.3 but 5 surprise songs)
3. Production quality (plenty of technically excellent 70s)

### Recommendation Strategy

> **Identify the musical properties that are disproportionately present in your 90+ songs, then find those properties inside underexplored genres.**

Instead of "user likes Soundtrack → recommend Soundtrack," try:
- "User likes distinctive creative identity → find Country with unusual arrangement + dramatic vocal"
- "User likes surprise songs → find Pop artists with one exceptional track that transcends their catalog"
- "User likes underground gems → find niche artists with strong musical identity"
