## The Corrected Hypothesis

> **You strongly favor music with a pronounced creative identity, where the arrangement, melody, performance, and production feel deliberately shaped toward a distinctive payoff.**

Not "production quality" — because plenty of technically excellent songs land at 70. The Country experiment proves this: *Kerosene*, *White Liar*, *Independence Day* are all professionally made. Your response was: **"Yeah, but where's the thing?"**

## For the Prediction Model

### What Actually Predicts 90+

1. **Distinctive creative identity** (genre is a proxy, not the cause)
2. **Artist familiarity** (1.86x enrichment for 10+ song artists)
3. **Artist-relative rating** (is this song exceptional for THIS artist?)
4. **Specific sub-characteristics within genres** (orchestration + dynamic contrast for Soundtrack; vocal showcase for Pop; lyrical complexity for Rap)

### What Doesn't Predict 90+

1. Genre alone (Country avg 67.5 but 8.1% 90+ rate)
2. Artist reputation (Maroon 5 avg 66.3 but 5 surprise songs)
3. Production quality (plenty of technically excellent 70s)

### Next Steps

1. **Enrich with Spotify audio features** — energy, danceability, valence, acousticness, instrumentalness — to test whether "distinctive creative identity" maps to measurable audio characteristics
2. **Build the 90+ classifier** — binary: "Will this become a favorite?"
3. **Cluster the 90+ songs** — discover taste modes (cinematic, vocal showcase, theatrical, etc.)
4. **Use the 50 surprises as training anchors** — these are your hidden rules
