Smart Recommender ⓘ 🎯 How recommendations work Picks come from a hand-curated catalog grouped into "If you love X…" categories built around artists & genres you rate highly. Each pick carries a reason — often citing your own score of a related song (e.g. "You rated Odo 96/100"). Songs you've already added, marked Listened, or banned are filtered out automatically so you only see fresh suggestions. Algorithm picks — the top section is the opposite: nothing hand-picked. Your rated genres & artist scores are turned into a taste vector and every song in an acclaim database is scored & ranked (genre affinity + artist affinity + acclaim), so you see only new, computed matches. 🔄 Fresh Recommendations re-checks the same pool against your latest collection — as you add/listen to songs, owned picks drop away and new ones surface. Connecting Spotify unlocks a separate ML engine that matches deeper audio features.
